Current root lint fails at `apps/api/shared/betterAuth.ts:273`. Older green records apply to earlier tree states.

5. **Functional limits**: selector explicitly returns nonadaptive state; bounded quotation candidates and deterministic facilitation do not establish conceptual generation, level adaptation or real model-quality misconception detection. Evidence-gated XP crash recovery, real LiveKit and thresholded instructor analytics are not established.
