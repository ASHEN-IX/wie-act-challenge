Derived from actual module mounts and route declarations. Nest AuthGuard verifies the Better Auth cookie session; original domain handlers retain their role, ownership, membership and subscription policies. Internal shared-secret requests remain subject to the domain contract and cannot acquire an authenticated browser identity. Public health/docs, billing config and signed webhook are exemptions. Actual authentication endpoints are hosted by Next/Better Auth and listed in AUTH_MIGRATION.md. Source links identify the exact policy implementation; a handler identity scope does not imply every route requires ownership of other users (leaderboards and friend discovery intentionally expose permitted data).
